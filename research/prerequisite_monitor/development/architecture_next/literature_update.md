# Stage B literature update — 2026-10-05 (Asia/Shanghai)

## Search driven by A, not by a presumed architecture

Search rounds were run 2026-10-05 against primary arXiv, PMLR, CVF, OpenReview and official project/code pages. Round 1 queries covered `video goal-conditioned progress / process reward`, `object-centric spatial relation before-after verification`, `robot skill precondition affordance`, `counterfactual feasibility alternative route`. Round 2 covered `wrong-object action credit`, `occlusion crop uncertainty`, `goal conditioned task progress before after`, and object removal. I followed ARS's cited progress/reasoning methods and local matrix leads, then checked ProcVLM, PRIMO R1, CoINS, SORNet and ReKep as competing mechanisms. Broad navigation, 3D control and generic VQA hits were excluded where their inference inputs or output targets did not match. Rounds 1–2 added adjacent work but no new category beyond event verification, process reward, relation/precondition prediction and counterfactual feasibility; this is a bounded saturation point, **not** an absence claim.

They support relation verification as an existing idea, while their extra views/state are outside the Monitor contract. Their presence narrows any novelty claim.

## Learned distinction

An observation such as “bar no longer over the opening” is a video-grounded relation. “The bar had to move to complete the final instruction” additionally requires ruling out acceptable alternate placements/grasps. A single RGB view cannot automatically establish that stronger claim, even with perfect relation extraction. This is why Stage A's scene-validity column stays separate from its model-evidence column. A model may reasonably answer `unknown` when the critical region is occluded or a route cannot be resolved, but an abstention with clearly visible conflicting endpoints is an error in evidence use, not improved accuracy.
