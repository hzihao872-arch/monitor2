01 · Method
Value, grounded in time.
RynnValue predicts absolute remaining time and signed temporal
displacement with frozen 256-bin quantile supports, while a language
head verifies instruction-video alignment. Value-isolation attention
suppresses shortcuts during training; inference uses a standard causal mask.
RynnValue scales timestamp-derived temporal-distance supervision from
roughly 3M instruction-conditioned clips to progress estimation, failure
detection, real-world reinforcement learning, and—demonstrated in the
paper—inference-time value guidance.
Training recipe: temporal sampling, order shuffling, instruction
mismatch augmentation, and value-isolation attention.
02 · Trajectory-ranking results
Generalizes beyond the training distribution.
RynnValue is evaluated on six out-of-distribution robot datasets.
The 8B model achieves the strongest average trajectory-ranking
result, while the ablation isolates the contribution of each design
component.
