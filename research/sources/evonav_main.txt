Figure 2. Overview framework of EvoNav. EvoNav addresses zero-shot VLN-CE with two complementary reasoning schemes: Future
Chain-of-Thought (F-CoT) and History Chain-of-Experience (H-CoE). Given panoramic RGB and depth images, a standard waypoint
predictor [20] first identifies candidate waypoint images. These images are then analyzed by the F-CoT, which predicts next-step actions
and landmarks as thoughts to guide current navigation progress and direction. After that, H-CoE retrieves trajectory- and scene-related
experience from a dynamic experience bank that continuously summarizes and stores completed trajectories and visited scenes. Finally,
the retrieved historical experiences and future thoughts are fused within the action prediction module to support decision-making.
