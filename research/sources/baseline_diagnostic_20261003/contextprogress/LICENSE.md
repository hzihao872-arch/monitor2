# License

ContextProgress-Bench combines two kinds of content.

**Videos** (`full_video.mp4`, `subtasks/*/video.mp4`) are unmodified frames re-cut from three public
robot-manipulation datasets. They remain under the terms of their sources:

- RMBench: https://github.com/robotwin-Platform/rmbench
- RoboDojo: https://huggingface.co/datasets/RoboDojo-Benchmark/RoboDojo
- LIBERO-Mem: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11478, built on LIBERO (https://libero-project.github.io/)

They are redistributed here for research use. Check each source's terms before any other use.

**Annotations** (episode selection, ground-truth subtask boundaries, rewritten main and subtask
instructions, `manifest.json`, `instruction.json`, `episodes.jsonl`, `subtasks.jsonl`,
`episode_id_mapping.json`) are by the ContextProgress-Bench authors and are released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
