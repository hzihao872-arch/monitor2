D     Failed Memory Construction
We construct the failure memory by extracting em-
beddings from the keyframes of each failure trajec-
tory (Algorithm 1), concatenating them into joint
vectors, and storing them with metadata in Faiss.

tion tasks, and a selection of these tasks is presented
in Figure 5.
